Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

The short version

  • MiniGo is a self-custodial wallet. Your recovery phrase and keys are made on your phone and stay there. We never see them.
  • MiniGo has no servers that collect your data and no accounts with us. Your name, email and phone number stay on your phone.
  • Before your backup is saved anywhere, it's encrypted on your phone with a password only you know.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or ads, and we don't sell data.
  • Payments are recorded on the public Stellar network, which anyone can read.

Who we are

MiniGo is a wallet for the Stellar network that also runs mini apps: small apps from other developers that open inside the wallet. This policy covers the MiniGo mobile app and this website, minigo.app. "We" and "MiniGo" mean the team that makes them.

MiniGo currently runs on Stellar's test network (testnet). Money there is test money with no real value.

What stays on your phone

This information is created and kept on your device. We don't receive it and couldn't give it to anyone.

  • Your recovery phrase and keys. Made on your phone and kept in its secure storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore). Where your phone supports it, the phrase is locked behind your fingerprint or face.
  • Your PIN. Kept only as a salted hash, never as the PIN itself.
  • Your profile. The name, email address and optional phone number you enter during setup. They show on your profile and aren't sent anywhere.
  • App data. Your settings, the list of mini apps you've connected, notes on payments made inside mini apps and which notifications you've read.

Your backup

When you back up your wallet, MiniGo encrypts your recovery phrase on your phone using a backup password you choose (scrypt and AES-256-GCM). The backup file holds only that encrypted phrase, your public wallet address and the settings needed to decrypt it. It holds no profile details.

The encrypted file is saved to your own cloud storage: a private app folder in your Google Drive on Android, or iCloud on iPhone. Until cloud sign-in is available in the app, the file stays on your phone.

We never see your backup password or your recovery phrase, so we can't reset the password or recover the backup for you.

Google user data

If you back up to Google Drive, MiniGo asks for one permission: drive.appdata. It lets MiniGo use a hidden folder in your Drive that only MiniGo can reach. MiniGo can't see or change any of your other Drive files.

  • MiniGo uses this access only to save, load and delete its own encrypted backup file, when you back up or restore.
  • The file goes straight between your phone and Google. It doesn't pass through any MiniGo server, and no person at MiniGo reads it.
  • We don't transfer, sell or use this data for advertising, or for any purpose other than your backup.

MiniGo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove MiniGo's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

What leaves your phone

To work, MiniGo talks to a few services run by other companies. Like any website you visit, each of them can see your IP address.

  • The Stellar network (servers run by the Stellar Development Foundation): your public wallet address to show your balance and history, and the transactions you sign. Everything on the Stellar network is public and permanent. Anyone can see an address's balance and payments, and no one can delete them.
  • CoinGecko: a request for the current XLM price, so MiniGo can show balances in dollars. It includes no information about you or your wallet.
  • Friendbot (Stellar's testnet faucet): your wallet address, only when you ask for test XLM.
  • Google Drive or iCloud: your encrypted backup file, as described above.

Some screens link to other websites, such as Stellar Expert for transaction details or Circle's faucet for test USDC. They open in your browser only when you tap them, under their own privacy policies.

Mini apps

Mini apps are websites made by other developers, shown inside MiniGo. Each has its own privacy policy, and what it does with information is up to it.

  • A mini app learns your public wallet address only after you connect it, and you can disconnect it at any time in Profile.
  • Every payment and signature a mini app asks for needs your approval with your PIN.
  • MiniGo blocks mini apps from using your camera, microphone and location.

Permissions on your phone

  • Camera: used only to scan payment QR codes. Images aren't saved or sent anywhere.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock: handled entirely by your phone's operating system. MiniGo only learns whether the check passed and never receives your fingerprint or face data.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, tracking or advertising in the app or on this website.
  • No accounts on MiniGo servers and no copy of your data held by us.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

This website

minigo.app uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google can see your IP address when the page loads. The site is static and doesn't collect anything you type or click.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any mini app in Profile.
  • Remove your wallet from a phone with "Forgot PIN?" on the lock screen, or by uninstalling MiniGo, which deletes the data MiniGo kept on that phone. Keep your recovery phrase or backup first, or you'll lose access to the wallet.
  • Delete a Google Drive backup by removing MiniGo's access in your Google Account, then deleting its hidden app data in Google Drive's settings.

Because we don't hold your personal data, we have nothing to send you or delete on our side. Records on the Stellar network can't be changed or deleted by anyone, including us.

Children

MiniGo isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Security

We design MiniGo so your keys never leave your phone unencrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your recovery phrase and backup password private and somewhere safe.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app or on this website.
